# Odyssey

Odyssey is a field journal and expedition network for slow travel across Southeast Asia.
It is a place to find routes worth taking, read the stories behind them, meet people who
know the landscape, and plan journeys that leave room for the unexpected.

## The Experience

Odyssey is built around five parts of an expedition:

- **Chronicles** - Read first-hand stories from mountains, islands, rivers, and remote roads.
- **Routes** - Explore practical, multi-day journeys with distances, surfaces, elevation,
	seasons, resupply notes, and places to stay.
- **Map** - Follow waypoints across the region, from mountain passes and temples to river
	moorings and family homestays.
- **Circle** - Discover companions, local knowledge, and people whose experience can make
	a journey more meaningful.
- **Gatherings** - Find shared expeditions and make plans with fellow travelers.

## What Odyssey Is For

Odyssey is designed for travelers who want more than a checklist of destinations. It brings
together the human stories, practical details, and sense of place that help an expedition
feel personal and grounded.

The project currently focuses on Southeast Asia, with routes and stories inspired by places
including Vietnam, Laos, Indonesia, and the Mekong region.

## Status

Odyssey is an evolving prototype with a curated atlas of routes, stories, companions, and
gatherings. Its visual language draws from expedition notebooks, archival maps, and the
quiet attention of a well-kept field journal.

## Try It

This is a Flutter app. With Flutter installed, run it on a connected device or emulator from
the project directory:

```bash
flutter run
```
